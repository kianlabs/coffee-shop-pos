# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) (licensed CC BY 4.0). This is a shorter restatement for Suro POS.

## Our pledge

We want everyone to be able to take part in this project, whatever their age, background, experience, gender, nationality, religion, or ability, and without harassment. We pledge to act in ways that keep the community open, welcoming, and healthy.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to the project or to coding.
- Give feedback that is specific and kind, and accept feedback the same way.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.
- Write in English or Bahasa Indonesia. Both are welcome in issues and pull requests.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or images, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an address or email, without permission.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained pressure on maintainers.
- Anything else that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and reviews) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

To report a violation, email **security@kianlabs.com** with the subject line `Code of Conduct`. This is currently the project's only published contact address. Please include links or screenshots and a short description of what happened. Reports are handled privately, and the reporter's identity is not shared without consent.

Maintainers aim to acknowledge a report within **7 days**.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, and pull requests that break this code, and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban a person from the project. Decisions will be explained to the people involved whenever it is safe to do so.
